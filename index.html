URBANISM

McGill Urbanism is a student club for anyone interested in urban studies, sustainable cities, public transit, municipal politics and access to housing. We give those students a place to meet, learn and explore how the places we live are planned, built and shared.

  • Access to housing

About the club

Why urbanism

83%

of Canadians live in urban areas. Cities are also where inequality is most prevalent, which can further marginalize vulnerable communities.

Montreal is an urban hub, which makes it a good place to study urbanism as a wider idea.

We encourage students to think about how the structure of a city shapes the communities in it, and how that structure can create systemic problems like homelessness, gentrification and inequity.

We also show students how accessible the city can be through public and active transit, and what it takes to keep it that way.

Line of the year

What we do

  1. Last semester

    A tour of the REM on the South Shore

    We toured the Brossard branch of the REM and visited its control centre. It showed how new urban rail systems support sustainability and access across Greater Montreal.

  2. Every fall

    Master's student panel

    We invite Master's students to talk about life after undergrad: the work, the programs and the internships. Members hear about careers in the field from people who have first-hand experience.

  3. Through the year

    Speakers, tours and socials

    Our events team plans talks, site visits and casual get-togethers, so members can learn about the city and about each other.

  4. Always

    A place to meet people

    Social connections are a big part of university. Meeting people who share your interests can lead to good friends and good ideas.
